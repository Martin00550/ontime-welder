The work, explained

Designed around the opening. Built around the brief.

The dimensions of an opening are only part of a gate or railing enquiry. Photos of the surrounding area help show the layout, existing supports and how the finished piece needs to fit. Include a sketch or reference image to explain the style you have in mind.

For handrails and fencing, identify the overall length, changes in height and the locations where the work will meet existing surfaces. If a section is being repaired or matched, photograph the existing detail as well as the damaged area.

Window and door covers are also part of the existing fabrication range. Confirm the opening dimensions and intended arrangement before requesting an estimate. Discuss mounting, finish expectations and any site or design requirements before agreeing on the fabrication scope.
