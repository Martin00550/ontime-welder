Welding Service Questions

Do you offer mobile welding in Chilliwack?

Yes. On-Time Welding Services provides mobile, on-site welding repairs in Chilliwack and serves Abbotsford, Langley, Surrey, Vancouver and Coquitlam. Call 604-791-3422 with the job location and repair details to confirm availability.

What types of welding and metal repairs do you offer?

Services include stick, MIG, flux core and aluminum welding, custom fabrication, gates and railings, trailer repairs, auto and marine repairs, and equipment repairs such as buckets, augers and boom lifts. See the welding services for details.

Can you weld aluminum?

Yes. Aluminum welding is available for repair and fabrication projects. Send photos and describe the part, its material and the damage so I can discuss the work with you.

Can you fabricate a custom gate or railing?

Yes. Custom metalwork includes gates, handrails, fencing, and window or door covers. Share the dimensions, a sketch or reference photos, and your location when requesting an estimate.

How do I get a welding estimate?

Call 604-791-3422 or email on-timewelding@hotmail.com for a free, no-obligation estimate. Include photos, dimensions, the type of metal if known, and your location for mobile work. Availability and the scope of the job can then be discussed.
