The work, explained

Start with the damage. Plan the repair.

A crack, worn working surface or damaged attachment needs more information than the equipment name alone. Share the make or model, the affected part and how the damage occurred, if known. Photos of the complete assembly help explain where the repair sits and how it can be reached.

The service range includes bucket and auger repairs, hardfacing, cracked weld repairs and boom lift equipment work. Hardfacing concerns working surfaces exposed to wear; a cracked weld presents a different repair question. Identify which problem you need addressed when requesting an estimate.

Ask about mobile welding when transporting the equipment is difficult. Access, condition and the requirements of the part must be reviewed before a repair is agreed. A welding enquiry is not a replacement for any inspection or equipment-specific approval your machine requires.

Your questions, answered.

01

Do you offer bucket and auger repairs?

Yes. Bucket repairs, auger repairs and hardfacing are listed services. The condition and requirements of the actual component need to be reviewed before agreeing on the work.

02

Can you come to the equipment?

Mobile welding is available by arrangement. Call with the equipment location and access details to confirm whether an on-site repair can be arranged.

03

Can you quote a cracked weld from a photo?

Photos are a useful starting point for a free estimate. The full extent of a crack, the material and any inspection requirements may need further assessment before the repair scope is confirmed.
