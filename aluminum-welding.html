The work, explained

A repair starts with knowing the material.

An aluminum part can look straightforward while its material and past repairs remain uncertain. If you know the alloy, thickness or original application, include that information with your enquiry. If you do not, say so; a closer assessment may be needed.

Aluminum repair and fabrication are offered alongside auto and marine repair enquiries. Describe whether you need an existing joint repaired, a damaged part assessed or a new piece fabricated. Those are different scopes of work and benefit from different photos and measurements.

For a fabricated component, provide a drawing or sketch with the key dimensions and how the part will be used. For a repair, photograph the complete part as well as the damage. This makes the first conversation more useful without assuming that every aluminum component is suitable for welding.

Do you offer aluminum welding in Chilliwack?

Yes. Aluminum repair and fabrication are On-Time Welding Services offerings. Call with your project details to discuss suitability and availability.

02

What if I do not know the aluminum alloy?

Include whatever information you have about the part and its original use. Do not guess the alloy; the material may need further assessment before work can be agreed.

Can you help with marine or auto parts?

Auto and marine repairs are listed services. Specify the actual part, damage and material so its repair requirements can be discussed; this is not a promise to repair every vehicle or marine component.
