The work, explained

From a single joint to a fabrication project.

MIG and flux core are available processes within the wider welding service. For a new fabrication job, explain what needs to be made, the measurements and how the parts will fit together. For a repair, show the existing joint and the damage around it.

Structural and production work are listed in the service description, but the actual requirements of each job still need to be established. Provide any drawings, specifications or inspection requirements that apply to your project when requesting an estimate.

You do not need to decide between MIG, flux core and stick welding before calling. The material, work location and joint requirements guide that discussion. For aluminum parts, use the dedicated aluminum welding page to explain the material and repair needs.

Yes. Both are On-Time Welding Services offerings. The appropriate setup depends on the actual material, joint and work conditions.

02
