Privacy & Browser Storage

What this website stores

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies. If you accept or decline the notice, it saves that choice in your browser's local storage under cookieConsent. This keeps the notice from appearing again in the same browser. The choice is not sent to On-Time Welding Services.

You can remove it by clearing site data in your browser. The notice will then appear again. The website can still be read if browser storage or JavaScript is disabled.

Images, styles and fonts

Images, styles and fonts are served by this website. The page does not need to contact Google Fonts, a styling CDN or an analytics provider to display its content. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and requested pages, to deliver the website.

Contact and external links

Phone and email links open your chosen phone or email application. Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp links take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. Contact details and project information you send by email or messaging are shared through the service you choose.
