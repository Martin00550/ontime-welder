The work, explained

Choose the process for the repair.

Stick welding is one of the available welding processes, alongside MIG, flux core and aluminum work. You do not need to choose the process before making an enquiry. Start with the part, its material and the problem you need to resolve.

Existing service examples include heavy machinery, tools and galvanized steel repair enquiries. Material condition, coatings and access to the joint all affect how a repair is approached. Describe those details and share photos rather than assuming that a process name alone defines the job.

For equipment work, include the machine and affected component. For a tool or smaller item, explain its original use and show the complete part. Mobile welding can be discussed where the work is located; availability and site conditions still need to be confirmed.

Before we get to work

Stick Welding.
Your questions, answered.

01

Do I need to specify stick welding when I call?

No. Describe the part, damage and intended use. The appropriate process can be discussed after reviewing the actual job.

02

Can you discuss galvanized steel repairs?

Yes. Galvanized steel repairs are listed in the existing stick-welding service range. Mention the coating and part details so preparation and repair requirements can be reviewed.

03

Is stick welding available on site?

On-site work can be discussed through the mobile welding service. Send the location and job details to confirm availability and a suitable setup.
