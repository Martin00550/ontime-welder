The work, explained

Bring the idea. Define the details.

A useful fabrication brief does not have to be a finished engineering drawing. Start with a sketch, the dimensions that matter and what the finished piece needs to do. Reference photos can help communicate the shape, but measurements are needed to discuss the actual build.

Custom metalwork includes brackets, light shades and trailer work. Describe how the piece will attach, what it must fit around and whether you are replacing an existing part. If there is a matching component, photograph it alongside the measurements.

Gates, railings, fencing and window or door covers have their own dedicated service page. Use that page for property metalwork and this one for individual parts or other fabrication enquiries. Materials, finish expectations and any design requirements should be discussed before the scope is agreed.

Before we get to work

Custom Metal Fabrication.
Your questions, answered.

01

Do I need a drawing to request fabrication?

A sketch, measurements and reference photos are a useful starting point. More detailed requirements may be needed for the actual build, depending on the part and its use.

02

Do you fabricate gates and railings?

Yes. Gates, handrails, fencing, and window or door covers are services. The gates and railings page explains what to include in that type of enquiry.

03

Can you copy or replace an existing metal part?

Send photos, dimensions and the intended use of the part to discuss the options. Whether a replacement can be made depends on the material, fit and requirements of the project.
